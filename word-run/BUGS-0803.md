## The finding that is not on your list

Six of your reports say the same sentence in different words:

> [5] three modals for one event · [27] robin AND the 3rd slot · [28] robin AND branch AND
> gold · [33] stars AND gifts · [40] gifts AND branch AND gold · [41] and a restless tile

**Nothing in the game limits how many new things a round may introduce.** Every subsystem
decides independently whether today is its day: the goal schedule, the obstacle spread, the
milestone roll, the seat opener, the friend queue, the tutorial. None of them can see the
others. On a bad level five of them fire at once.

This is the same disease as the ice: independent authors, no arbiter. It is the single
highest-value fix in the list, because it makes six reports disappear at once and prevents
the next six.

## The plan

Ordered by damage per hour of work, not by your list order.

### Step 1 - Stop the lying (P0, small, no design questions)
Score [17,23,26] and word validation [13,14,15,25,37]. Both are single-source-of-truth
problems with no open design questions. A game that misreports your score and steals your
word is not testable at all. **Nothing else should be touched first.**

### Step 2 - The novelty budget (P0 structural, medium)
One arbiter that decides what a round is allowed to introduce, and a hard cap of **one new
thing per round**. Goal, obstacle debut, milestone, seat opening, friend arrival and tutorial
all request through it; the rest defer to the next eligible round. Kills [5,27,28,33,40,41]
and prevents the class.

### Step 3 - Verify every pet (P0/P1, medium)
A per-friend harness that fires each power on a seeded board and asserts what it did.
Confirms or kills [16,22,36,39,42], and catches the 37 unwired powers. This must be
mechanical - your list already shows my reading of the code is not reliable enough here.

### Step 4 - The pet system (P1, large)
`PETS-SPEC.md` Option A, which carries [8,18,20,21,27,31,34,45] and the ceremony ordering in
[44]. Starting with the state machine fixes [45] and the purchase bug on its own.

### Step 5 - Ceremony choreography (P1, medium)
Scrim rule, beat consolidation, no auto-dismiss on anything a player must read. [1,2,5,7,9,10,19]

### Step 6 - Copy sweep (P1, medium)
Every player-facing string against one rule: actionable without a glossary. [3,4,24,35,43]

### Step 7 - The rest (P2)
[6,11] seeding, [12] input, [38] branch reward.
