The door — the country is the cover

The hand-drawn evening is gone: no sky gradient, no sun, no clouds, no treeline, no hill, no friends peeking over it. The country's own painting fills the door, unveiled. The wordmark is now eight tiles in two rows and the subtitle plate is retired. One thing still has to be solved — the chapter block at the foot, which is cream type sitting directly on paint that ranges from near-black caves to bright snow.

Why this needs deciding rather than eyeballing. Measured across all fifteen paintings at the exact height the chapter block sits: cream type straight on the painting bottoms out at 1.57:1 — that is not a close call, it is illegible on the pale countries.
