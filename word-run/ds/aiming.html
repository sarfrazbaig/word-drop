Aiming a letter, with a thumb in the way

Press and slide has always worked. Pointer-down raises the preview and the preview follows your finger while it is down. The game has simply never said so, and on a phone the thing meant to show you where the letter will land is directly underneath the finger doing the aiming.

Two separate problems, and they need different answers. One is DISCOVERY: nobody knows the gesture exists, because on a tap the preview lives for about a tenth of a second. The other is OCCLUSION: even once you know, you cannot see the landing cell because your own thumb is on it. A column light solves neither well and looks unfinished, so it is out. Below are four that do not fill an area.
What is not in question. Discovery gets fixed in the tutorial either way: one line on the screen where the drop is already being taught, plus the coaching hand performing the slide instead of pointing at a column. That is copy and animation, not a visual system, so it is not what these options are about.

These four are only about the aiming feedback itself, and they can be combined. A and C in particular are the pair most mobile games ship together: the piece rides your thumb so you feel you are holding it, and a thin guide says exactly where it lands.
